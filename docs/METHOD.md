# Method and interpretation

The primary signal is the negative provider risk-premium score. A fixed fiscal/external/growth comparison is evaluated separately. Monthly snapshots choose the latest available record, then apply quality and quote-age rules; a newer rejected record must not resurrect an older acceptable value.

The primary horizon is 63 business days; 21 and 126 days are sensitivity horizons. Forecasts use annual expanding training, exclude labels unfinished at the test-year boundary, and standardize with training observations. Paired evaluation uses the same month and country sample and relative cross-country outcomes.

The recorded primary study used synchronized 6- and 12-month blocks and simultaneous intervals across its primary comparisons. It did not establish robust prediction improvement. The synthetic monthly-panel demo validates prediction plumbing and deliberately planted relationships; it does not reproduce the empirical study.

Provider score/component vintages remain unverified. CDS and bond outcomes have different definitions, and bonds retain rate/duration risk. Screening gaps are hypotheses, not current investment recommendations or validated trading P&L. Source records and detailed empirical outputs are excluded.
